Morning light reaches the desk, turning ordinary paper into a small field of possibility. A notebook waits beside a cup of tea, open to a clean page. Outside, traffic hums, neighbors talk, and the day begins without ceremony. I write one sentence, then another, letting ideas arrive before judgment can interrupt them. Some thoughts are useful, some are playful, and some simply make room for better ones. By noon, the page holds a map of questions, plans, and tiny discoveries. None of it needs to be perfect. It only needs to exist, patiently inviting the next thing to begin today.
